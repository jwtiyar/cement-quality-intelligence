OPC Correlation Matrix

Pearson linear correlation coefficient (r). +1 indicates direct relationship; -1 indicates inverse relationship.

How to read this matrix (range restriction & Bogue anticoupling)

Correlations are weak in absolute terms because the plant operates inside tight quality windows (range restriction) — expect |r| well below 1 even for real effects. C3S hydrates in days and drives early strength; C2S hydrates slowly and matters mostly beyond 28 days. C3S and C2S are mathematically anticoupled by the Bogue equations (r ≈ -0.96 to -0.99), so treat their rows as one signal, not two independent ones. Switch cement type above — products with different clinker targets (SRC, SBC) legitimately show different signs.
